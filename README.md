# marine-sales-service-data-platform

## Overview

An end-to-end data engineering project that transforms quotations, purchase orders, and invoices into structured data for pricing, sales analysis, and quotation automation.

The goal is to replace manual document-based workflows with a modern data platform that enables automation and business intelligence.

## Why I Started This Project

I worked as a public elementary school teacher in South Korea for four years. Public school teaching is widely regarded as a stable and desirable career in Korea, offering strong job security, generous holidays, and a healthy work-life balance. However, I decided to leave that stability behind and move to Singapore to pursue a dream I had carried since childhood: becoming an IT engineer.

Studying an entirely new field in a language other than my native language was not easy. With the unwavering support of my husband, who continued to believe in my potential, I was able to complete my degree in Computer Science.

My husband runs a marine parts service company. As I observed its day-to-day operations, I noticed that valuable information was scattered across quotations, purchase orders, invoices, and other business documents. I began to see an opportunity to use data engineering to transform these fragmented records into structured, reliable data—reducing repetitive work and supporting better business decisions.

That realization became the starting point of this project. I wanted to apply what I had learned to solve a genuine business problem and create something that could make a meaningful difference to the person who had supported me throughout my journey.

This project is both my first gift to my husband and my first step in building my career as a data engineer.


## Project Goal

This project aims to build an end-to-end data platform for a marine parts service company.

The system will:
- Extract data from quotations, purchase orders, and invoices
- Transform and load the data into a relational database
- Support historical price and sales analysis
- Generate quotation files
- Provide a chatbot interface for business queries and quotation creation
